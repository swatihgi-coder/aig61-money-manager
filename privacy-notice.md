# MoneyManager Privacy Notice

*Last updated: November 1, 2026*

MoneyManager helps adults in the United States understand their finances, plan for their goals and, if they choose, connect with eligible financial service providers. This notice explains what personal information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how long we keep it and the choices available to you.

MoneyManager is the operator responsible for the personal information described in this notice. In this notice, “MoneyManager,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to that operator. This notice covers the MoneyManager app, its financial questionnaire, related planning and optional matching features, and support and privacy requests concerning those services.

Privacy contact: MoneyManager Privacy Team. Use the Privacy request form in the app’s Privacy section or the public version of that form linked from the sign-in page.

Our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy appears after this notice and is separately accessible through the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy link on the service homepage and in the app’s Privacy section. It explains our handling of health, tobacco and family-planning information and related inferences. Where that policy provides greater protection for consumer health data, it controls.

## 1. Your choices at a glance

* Core planning uses your questionnaire answers to organize your finances and generate financial and insurance-needs profiles. The questionnaire identifies the information required for that service.
* Child details, plans to have more children, health-insurance details, tobacco-use answers and interest in provider matching are optional. You can use core planning without providing these optional answers or enabling matching.
* Household information is private by default. A spouse or household administrator does not receive automatic access. Each adult controls the categories they allow another adult to see.
* Provider matching is off by default. A named provider receives a limited, previewed summary only after you separately opt in through MoneyManager and directly with that provider.
* We do not give matched providers your raw questionnaire answers, health information or child-identifying information. We do not give insurers access to your information through the service.

This notice explains our practices. Reading it or using MoneyManager does not, by itself, constitute consent to optional matching, household sharing or sensitive-data processing that requires your separate consent.

## 2. Personal information we collect

We collect the information you enter in the questionnaire, the records we create from those answers, and the limited records needed to provide and protect the service. Some information concerns you; some may concern a spouse or child you are authorized to include. The following categories describe the questionnaire, rather than permission to collect additional information from other sources.

The questionnaire does not ask for bank login credentials, full payment-card numbers, government identification numbers, copies of wills or trusts, or medical records. Please do not put those details, or unnecessary information about another person, in free-text fields.

The service described here uses information entered into MoneyManager rather than connected bank transaction feeds. We do not use questionnaire information to train external artificial intelligence models or permit contracted processors to use it for their own model training.

## 3. Where information comes from

Information comes directly from you, from an authorized adult who provides information about you, and from a parent or legal guardian who provides information about a child. We also generate profiles and estimates from questionnaire answers, permission records from your choices, and limited service records from requests and system events. Providers supply criteria describing the types of financial needs they serve; those criteria help us identify potential matches internally.

Only submit another adult’s information with their permission. Each adult must provide their own consent where required, including for health information. One spouse’s agreement does not replace the other’s. Children’s information must be supplied by an authorized parent or guardian. A person whose information was supplied by someone else may contact us to access, correct or request deletion of their information without opening a new account.

## 4. How we use information

* Provide financial planning: organize identity, contact, household, financial and estate-planning inputs; explain your financial situation; calculate financial profiles and insurance needs; and support your goals.
* Provide optional planning features: use child and family-planning information for family and education planning, and optional health-insurance and tobacco answers for internal insurance planning, with the required permissions and consent.
* Manage household access: record and enforce each adult’s selected permissions, invitations and withdrawals, with separate restrictions for child and sensitive information.
* Provide optional matching: compare financial needs with eligible non-insurer providers’ criteria, generate potential matches internally and manage the limited disclosures you specifically authorize.
* Operate and protect the service: respond to requested support, troubleshoot problems, control access, investigate security incidents and keep necessary records of these activities. We minimize and mask financial and health content in operational records.
* Honor privacy choices and legal duties: verify and respond to requests, record consent and withdrawal, correct or delete information, and retain limited evidence where required for a specific legal duty, security need or legal claim.

We do not use the information for unrelated marketing, advertising profiles or cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money or other valuable consideration, including through provider matching. We do not use or disclose personal information to train large language models. A provider opt-in does not authorize a sale or any use outside the disclosed purpose.

## 5. Financial profiles and automated calculations

MoneyManager uses calculations and rules to organize the information you provide. Relevant inputs include income, assets and balances, contributions and rates of return, debts and interest rates, housing and mortgage costs, existing insurance, education costs, financial preferences, goals and retirement plans. Insurance-needs estimates use the financial obligations and insurance amounts you supply. Optional information is used only for the relevant optional planning feature.

Matching compares your financial needs and selected goals with criteria supplied by eligible providers. We do not use these profiles to decide credit or insurance eligibility or pricing. The profiles are planning outputs and may be incomplete or inaccurate if the underlying answers are incomplete or inaccurate. We do not independently verify every financial entry. Our formulas are proprietary, but you may ask us to explain the information and criteria relevant to an output concerning you.

You can correct your inputs and ask us to review a disputed output through the privacy contact or request route above. Human review is available for consequential errors. We will explain any correction or why an output remains unchanged.

## 6. Household access and provider matching

### Household access

Each adult must accept an invitation and choose which categories another adult may access. Sensitive information and child information require separate restricted permissions. You may revoke access at any time through the app’s privacy controls or by contacting us. Revocation blocks future access through MoneyManager; it cannot retrieve a copy someone lawfully received earlier.

### Matching before you identify yourself to a provider

When you enable matching, we compare needs and provider criteria inside MoneyManager. Before the required opt-ins, any output released to providers is limited to genuinely deidentified group or category suggestions. It does not contain your name, a persistent user identifier or a linkable individual record. We exclude child identity, family plans, tobacco information, health and insurance answers, and free-text content from outbound matching attributes.

### Sharing a named summary

Before sharing a summary linked to your name, we identify the particular eligible non-insurer provider, explain the purpose and show you the exact summary. You must opt in separately through MoneyManager and directly with that provider. Only after both permissions are recorded do we disclose the previewed summary of your chosen goals and broad financial bands. We do not send your underlying questionnaire, exact raw financial entries, health information, family plans, child identity or free-text answers.

The provider is responsible for information it processes independently under its own notice. Review that notice before agreeing. Your permission is specific to the named recipient and purpose. You can withdraw through MoneyManager’s privacy controls or contact us. Withdrawal stops future optional processing or disclosure covered by that permission, and we notify recipients where required. It does not automatically erase a recipient’s records that the law permits or requires it to retain. Refusing or withdrawing matching does not prevent core planning.

## 7. Other access and disclosures

### Contracted service providers

We use providers for hosting, databases, backups, account security, transactional messages and support, only as needed. They receive only information necessary for their assigned work. Hosting and backup providers may process stored questionnaire and profile information; security providers process necessary security and access records; messaging providers process contact details and the content needed for service messages; support providers process the limited information needed for your case.

These providers must follow our instructions and contractual confidentiality, security, subprocessor, incident-assistance and return or deletion requirements. They may not use your information for their own advertising, sales or model training. A contracted technical provider is different from a financial provider you choose through optional matching.

### Authorized personnel

Staff access is limited by role and need. Support access requires your request, is limited to the case and is logged. Engineering uses masked test information; exceptional production access requires approval, time limits and logging. Privacy and security personnel access only what is needed to respond to rights requests or incidents.

### Legal requirements

We may disclose limited information when required by valid legal process or otherwise specifically permitted by applicable law to address a security incident or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. We assess the request, restrict the disclosure to what is legally justified and apply any additional protections required for sensitive or consumer health data. This provision does not authorize routine marketing disclosure or insurer access.

## 8. Deidentified information

We may create group-level or category-level information that cannot reasonably be linked to you. We maintain reasonable safeguards against reidentification, commit to maintaining and using deidentified information in deidentified form, and do not attempt to reidentify it except as permitted by law to test our safeguards. Recipients must comply with contractual restrictions against reidentification. A pseudonym or coded user profile is not treated as deidentified merely because your name was removed. A later name-linked disclosure comes from personal information retained within MoneyManager and requires the separate permissions described above.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising pixels or advertising analytics in the service described in this notice. We use essential session identifiers to keep you signed in and protect your session, and local storage to remember privacy choices. Session identifiers expire when the session ends; stored choices remain until you change them, clear the relevant storage or close your account. Server-side consent records follow the retention rules below. You may manage available browser or device storage controls. Disabling a technology needed for a requested feature may prevent that feature from working.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no such activity to stop when a Global Privacy Control or other legally recognized opt-out signal is received. A signal does not authorize optional provider matching and does not replace your separate sharing choices. We do not change our practices solely in response to a browser’s legacy Do Not Track setting.

## 10. How long we keep information

We keep questionnaire answers and derived profiles only while needed for the services you request. We delete them following a verified deletion request or account closure, subject to a specific exception permitted by applicable law. If an account becomes inactive without a deletion request, we apply a maximum retention period of 24 months from your last use of the service, with earlier deletion when the information is no longer needed. We review sensitive information sooner and remove it when its purpose ends or required consent is withdrawn, subject to applicable law.

We keep permission, support, security and privacy-request evidence only for as long as needed to enforce your choices, resolve a request or dispute, address security, or meet an identified legal requirement. We consider the purpose, sensitivity, applicable legal period and whether less information will suffice. We review continued necessity regularly. Information retained for a specific legal hold is restricted and separated from ordinary service use; it is not retained indefinitely under a general exception.

Deleted information in backups expires through the applicable backup cycle and must not be restored to active use. Deleted information is removed from backups within 90 days after the deletion request is authenticated or the applicable retention period ends. If a backup is restored for recovery, deletion instructions are reapplied. Shorter or more specific legal deadlines control. The separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains the additional rules for the deletion of health data.

The questionnaire’s possible 20-year financial history describes the period covered by your answers. It does not mean that we keep your information for 20 years. Retention restrictions continue to apply after you stop using MoneyManager.

## 11. Security and processing location

We protect personal information using encryption in transit and at rest, controlled encryption keys, staff multifactor authentication, restricted role-based access, access reviews, secure development practices, vulnerability testing and incident-response procedures. We assess service providers before allowing access and periodically afterward. Encryption does not mean that every authorized hosting or support operator is technically unable to access information.

Our storage, replicas, backups and authorized processor support access are located in the United States. We require these location restrictions in supplier arrangements. No security measure guarantees complete protection. Contact us promptly if you believe your MoneyManager information or account has been compromised.

## 12. Your privacy rights and requests

We provide access, correction, deletion, export and withdrawal options to all US users. Depending on your state and the law that applies, you may also have rights to:

* Confirm whether we process your information and obtain information about its categories, sources, uses and recipients, together with a copy of the information itself.
* Correct inaccuracies, request deletion and obtain a portable copy in a reasonably usable format.
* Obtain a list of specific third parties or categories of recipients to whom information has been disclosed, where applicable law provides that right.
* Opt out of sales, targeted advertising or certain profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and limit certain uses or disclosures of sensitive information. We do not undertake those sales, advertising or eligibility decisions; you can separately decline or withdraw optional matching and sensitive-data consent.
* Use an authorized agent, appeal a refusal, and exercise rights without unlawful discrimination or retaliation.

Submit a request using the Privacy request form in the app’s Privacy section or its public version linked from the sign-in page. Describe the request and enough information for us to locate the relevant record. Do not send account passwords, complete financial account numbers or unnecessary health details. You do not need to create a new account. We may ask you to authenticate through an existing account or provide proportionate information to protect your records and other people’s privacy. Information collected solely for verification is used only for that purpose and related security.

An authorized agent may use the same methods. We may request evidence of authority and, where permitted, confirmation directly from you. A parent or legal guardian may submit a request concerning a child’s information, subject to verification of that authority. If you need an accessible format or assistance submitting a request, contact us using the same details.

We respond without undue delay and within the period required by applicable law. For most access, correction and deletion requests, this is 45 days from receipt. If an extension is legally permitted and necessary, we explain the reason and revised deadline within the initial period. Shorter deadlines apply where required, including to certain withdrawal or opt-out requests. Requests are generally free; any legally permitted fee or refusal for excessive or unfounded requests will be explained.

If we deny a request, we explain the reason, any applicable exception and how to appeal. Send an appeal through the same request form, selecting “Privacy appeal”. We provide a written appeal decision within 45 days, or sooner where required. If an appeal is denied, we provide information on contacting the relevant state attorney general or other competent authority. We do not retaliate for exercising rights. A feature may become unavailable if you ask us to delete information necessary to provide it, but optional matching is not a condition of core planning.

## 13. Additional California disclosures

A category label does not expand the fields we collect beyond section 2.

Sources are described in section 3, purposes in sections 4–6, and recipients in sections 6–7. Relevant categories may be disclosed to contracted service providers only as needed for their work and to household members only under the selected permissions. An independently acting matched provider receives only the name-linked summary described in section 6. Retention periods or criteria for these categories appear in section 10.

Our current policy is not to sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, including information about people under 16. We use sensitive information only for the requested services and the restricted purposes described here, with any legally required consent. You may request limitation where California law provides that right, in addition to withdrawing optional consent. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing outside the specific matching permission you choose. California residents may also contact us about disclosures covered by California’s Shine the Light law.

Use section 12 to exercise your rights to know, access, correct, delete, opt out where applicable, limit sensitive-data use where applicable, or act through an authorized agent. We protect information about others when responding and explain any applicable legal exemption. These protections are not waived by using MoneyManager.

## 14. Children and changes to this notice

MoneyManager accounts are for adults aged 18 or older. Children do not hold accounts. Adults may optionally provide child information for family or education planning as described above. We do not knowingly collect questionnaire answers directly from children. If you believe a child has created an account or supplied information directly, contact us so we can investigate and remove the information as appropriate.

We update this notice when our practices change and show the updated date at the beginning. For material changes, we provide an appropriate in-app or other direct notice. Before materially changing a use or disclosure that requires consent, we explain the change and obtain that consent. Continuing to use the app does not replace consent required by law. Questions about this notice may be directed to the MoneyManager Privacy Team through the request form described above.

# MoneyManager Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

*Last updated on November 1, 2026*

This model describes MoneyManager’s proposed service design. Its controls, retention rules and request channels are illustrative commitments for the demonstration, rather than claims about a live service.

MoneyManager is the operator responsible for the practices described here. This separate policy explains how we collect, use and disclose consumer health data in connection with MoneyManager’s optional planning features, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to a person and reveals physical, mental or reproductive health status, including relevant inferences. It addresses the Washington My Health My Data Act and other applicable state consumer health data laws, including Nevada law.

Privacy contact: MoneyManager Privacy Team. Requests and appeals may be submitted through the Privacy request form in the app’s Privacy section or its public version linked from the sign-in page.

## 1. Consumer health data and sources

The categories of consumer health data we collect are:

* Health-insurance information you choose to provide: whether you have coverage, provider, plan type, premium, term, group/individual/marketplace status, and HMO/PPO or other plan type, to the extent it reveals health information.
* Whether you use tobacco, if you choose to answer.
* Whether you plan to have more children and how many, and other optional family information, to the extent it reveals reproductive or other health information.
* Health-related information you voluntarily include in free-text goals or notes. Please avoid providing medical records or health details that the questionnaire does not request.
* Inferences about health-related planning or insurance needs that we generate from those inputs, together with identifiers needed to associate the information with the correct person and enforce their choices.

These data come from you, from another adult who has permission to supply your information, from an authorized parent or guardian concerning a child, and from inferences generated inside MoneyManager. Each adult must give their own required consent; a spouse cannot supply that consent on their behalf. We do not obtain consumer health data from healthcare providers or connected bank feeds for these features.

## 2. Why and how we process consumer health data

We use optional health-insurance and tobacco answers for internal insurance planning and profiling. Optional family information supports family and education planning; it may inform an internal needs category for optional matching, but family or health information and health-revealing inferences are excluded from provider disclosures. We associate these data with the relevant profile, calculate planning outputs, display them to the authorized person and enforce access, withdrawal and deletion choices.

We collect and process optional consumer health data only with the affirmative consent required by applicable law. Any consent to share is separate from consent to collect. You may decline these optional features and still use core financial planning. We do not treat acceptance of a general privacy notice or terms as the required health-data consent.

## 3. Consumer health data disclosures

Our contracted hosting, database, backup and security processors may process the consumer health data and associated identifiers necessary to store and protect the service. A contracted support processor may access limited relevant information when you request support. These processors act only under our instructions and contractual restrictions; they cannot use the information for their own advertising, sale or model training.

A household adult can receive only the consumer health data categories you specifically select, with the separate permission and any consent required by law. Household status does not itself grant access. We do not share consumer health data with affiliates. We may make a narrowly limited disclosure if specifically required or permitted by applicable law; this does not authorize routine disclosure to insurers or matched providers.

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not disclose it to matched financial providers, insurers, advertising networks or data brokers. We do not send health-revealing individual profiles under the label “anonymous.” We do not use geofencing around healthcare facilities to identify or track people seeking care, collect their consumer health data, or send health-related messages or advertisements.

## 4. Your health data rights

You may ask us to confirm whether we collect, share or sell consumer health data about you and to access that data. Where required by applicable law, we provide a list of the third parties and affiliates that received it and an active email address or other online contact method for those recipients. You may withdraw consent to collection and sharing and request deletion. You may also request correction through the same contact route.

Submit requests or withdraw consent through the Privacy request form in the app’s Privacy section or its public version linked from the sign-in page. You do not need to create an account. We may use an existing account or request limited information reasonably necessary to authenticate your identity and authority. We do not request unrelated health details for verification. We stop the optional processing covered by a withdrawal and notify relevant recipients where required.

We respond without undue delay and no later than 45 days after receipt, or earlier if applicable law requires. Where legally permitted and reasonably necessary, we may extend once by 45 days, explaining the reason within the first 45 days. Authentication does not restart that period. Responses are free within applicable statutory limits; we explain any legally permitted fee or refusal for excessive or unfounded requests.

If we refuse a request, you may appeal through the same methods, selecting “Health data appeal”. We explain our decision in writing within 45 days of receiving the appeal, or earlier where required. If we deny the appeal, we provide a way to contact the relevant attorney general. Washington consumers may submit a complaint at https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint. We do not retaliate against you for exercising these rights.

## 5. Deletion and retention

We keep consumer health data only while needed for the optional purpose you authorize and review that need regularly. Inactive-account questionnaire data and profiles are subject to a maximum of 24 months from last service use, with earlier removal when no longer needed or when deletion or withdrawal requires it. A specific legal retention obligation applies only to the extent the governing health-data law permits it.

When we authenticate a valid deletion request, we delete the consumer health data from our records and notify the affiliates, processors, contractors and other recipients that must honor it. This includes relevant derived health information. We remove deleted information from backups within 90 days of authentication, or sooner where required. Washington law’s six-month ceiling for permitted archival or backup delays does not extend our shorter policy period. Data awaiting backup deletion is restricted and is not restored to active use without reapplying deletion instructions.

We disclose additional consumer health data categories or purposes and obtain the required affirmative consent before beginning the new collection, use or sharing. We update the date of this policy when it changes. Contact the MoneyManager Privacy Team through the request form above for questions or assistance.
